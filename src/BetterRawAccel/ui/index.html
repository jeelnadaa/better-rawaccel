General notes

hover or focus any control for its note
  1. ModeClick a tile or press 1–8. Tiles share one scale, so shapes compare directly. Each mode keeps its own values.
  2. Exact valueType a number; Enter applies, Esc cancels. ↑↓ step, Shift ×10, wheel while focused. Typed values may go past the ruler.
  3. RulerClick to jump, drag to set. Shift-drag for 1/10 speed. Double-click resets to default ▾. Focused: ←→, Home/End.
  4. ScrubDrag a parameter's name sideways to scrub its value without aiming at the ruler.
  5. Live markerMove the mouse: the ring and crosshair show your hand speed on the curve, with exact values on the axes. ▲ holds your peak for 3 s.
  6. Dashed curveWhat the driver has now. It appears while you have unwritten edits; the status line counts them.
  7. Write to driverButton or Ctrl+Enter. The driver waits one second before using new settings; the pen re-traces the curve when it lands.
  8. UndoCtrl+Z and Ctrl+Y step through edits. × next to the status drops everything not yet written.
  9. Profile & deviceTitle block: keep several profiles, and give a mouse its own DPI, polling rate or profile.
  10. Driver, sheet & typeDriver opens start-up, MCP, update and import/export settings. Sheet and Type change the look. ? opens these notes.
