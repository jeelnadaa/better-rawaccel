General notes

hover or focus any control for its note
  1. ModeClick a tile or press 1–6. Tiles share one scale, so shapes compare directly.
  2. Exact valueType a number; Enter applies, Esc cancels. ↑↓ step, Shift ×10, wheel while focused.
  3. RulerClick to jump, drag to set. Shift-drag for 1/10 speed. Double-click resets to default ▾.
  4. Ruler, keyboardTab to it, then ←→ step, Shift ×10, Home/End for the limits.
  5. ScrubDrag a parameter's name sideways to scrub its value without aiming at the ruler.
  6. Live markerMove the mouse: the ring and crosshair show your speed on the curve, with exact values on the axes.
  7. Peak holdThe ▲ on the x axis and the last readout hold your fastest input for 3 s.
  8. Dashed curveWhat the driver has now. It appears while you have unwritten edits.
  9. Write to driverButton or Ctrl+Enter anywhere. The pen re-traces the curve when it lands.
  10. Sheet & typeTheme and typeface switchers in the title block; your choice is remembered. ? opens these notes.
